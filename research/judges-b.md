### 1. Sankeerth Rao Karingula, PhD (Agentin AI, YC W25)
- **Role and background:** Founder and CEO. He has a PhD in Machine Learning from UC San Diego and studied EE at IIT Bombay. He was previously a Research Scientist at Google (per the [YC page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/agentin-ai)). He is a UCSD alum, so he is local and likely invested in the UCSD community. Team of 2, based in SF, with Harj Taggar as YC partner.
- **Company:** Agentin AI is a foundation-model research lab. The launch post says it builds "foundation models designed to work on problems without known answers," meaning models that "explore ideas, form hypotheses, test approaches, and build on their findings." The [site](https://agentin.ai) says "training foundational models and conducting research." Older third-party listings called it "enterprise workflow agents," so the company has pivoted to research.
- **Likely values:** Technical depth, agents that reason on open-ended problems, evaluation rigor, and real hypothesis-test loops instead of scripted flows. He will probably be skeptical of wrapper demos.
- **Angle and likely question:** Pitch diagnosis as hypothesis testing. The agent forms a hypothesis ("capacitor or fan motor?"), asks the user to run a test ("does the fan spin by hand?"), and updates its belief. Likely questions: "How does it handle a failure with no known answer?" and "How do you eval diagnosis accuracy?" Have a small eval ready, for example 20 labeled appliance failures with a hit rate. Also be ready to say how the agent knows when it is stuck and escalates to a human.
- **Win him:** Show a visible reasoning loop, with the agent changing its mind after a user-run test, plus one real accuracy number.
