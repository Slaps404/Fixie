Bigger impact · more seamless (ranked)

  1. Live camera "take a look" as the default when the caller agrees. It removes the hardest step for seniors: reading a model number.
  2. Fixie remembers the caller: "Hi Dorothy, is this about the dryer?" Caller ID + saved appliances means zero re-explaining and answers the scam objection.
  3. A real delivery trigger: a carrier tracking webhook (EasyPost/AfterShip) fires the install call, instead of a button press.
  4. Caregiver loop: co-sign over $50 by text, plus a "Mom's dryer is fixed, $13 vs $200" call. That's the person who pays and refers.
  5. "Fixed, or we pay the pro": the outcome guarantee is the business model. Every confirmed fix tunes the diagnosis odds (a data moat).
  6. Judges can call it: a public number with per-caller caps (1 job, 3 outbound calls, roster-only suppliers).
