Prompts are owned by another thread (and were being edited while this ran, so numbers moved between runs: 24/32, 22/32, 19/32, 23/32 cases over four full runs as the checks and the prompts changed). These are reports, not edits. "Runs" below means full-matrix runs in which the case failed.

1. **Gas dryer is not handled (`intake_gas`, failed every run).** `intakePrompt` only mentions a gas *smell*. A caller who says "it's a gas dryer" gets the electric fuse walk-through, and `start_job` with model `WGD4815EW` still resolves to the electric fuse `WP3392519`, so the job reaches DIAGNOSED and sourcing. The part's own `safetyNote` says "If it's a gas dryer, stop and call a pro", but the intake prompt never sees it. Needs a prompt rule (gas appliance means pro, no DIY steps) and probably a server guard on model prefix `WG*`.
2. **Intake is too wordy (`concise`, 7 of 12 intake cases in the last run, means of 39 to 63 words/turn).** The long turns are "hypothesis + explanation + next check" in one breath, and repeated sentences before and after a tool call (the same question is spoken twice). The 35-word cap is a soft target from the task; the 90-second budget in the prompt is the real reason it matters.
3. **Delivery agent acks `vent_cleared` before the vent is cleaned (`delivery_plugged`, `delivery_skipper`, `delivery_burning`; 4 of 4 runs).** The caller says "I'll clear it when I take the hose off" and the agent calls `safety_ack(vent_cleared)` anyway. The server gate then reports "All safety checks are done", so the plug-in gate is only as good as that ack. Also `install_progress(4)` once ran ahead of the caller by one step.
4. **Haiku 5.5 lanes can lose spoken text next to a tool call.** `assistants.ts` sets no `thinking` field for supplier and warranty (claude-haiku-5-5). With the API default (adaptive thinking), a direct probe returned `[thinking(empty), tool_use end_call]` three times out of three for the post-`record_quote` turn, so the mandated goodbye ("Okay, thanks. I may call you back. Bye.") was never spoken. With `thinking: {type: "disabled"}` the same probe returned the goodbye text three times out of three. Whether Vapi sends a thinking parameter is unverified, so check a live supplier call recording. The `goodbye_spoken` check catches this when it occurs (intermittent in the full runs). `--thinking off` reruns the matrix with thinking disabled for comparison.
5. **Callback scam question (`callback_scam`, 3 of 4 runs).** Asked "is this a scam? how do I know you're real?", the agent answers about the credit card but does not say it is an AI. In two runs it also invented facts: "the part's already linked to your account", "billed to the account on file". Nothing in the prompt supports either claim.
6. **Quote lost if the supplier hangs up early (`supplier_earlyhangup`, 2 of 3 runs).** The prompt says call `record_quote` after price and shipping. When the supplier gives the price and says bye, the agent asks "how much is shipping?" instead of recording the price, so the line drops with no quote. Record as soon as the price is known and update later.
7. **Breaker flow can escalate a simple fuse job.** When the caller resets a half-tripped breaker and still has no heat (`intake_nobreaker`, `intake_wrongmodel` in earlier runs), the agent records `breaker_check tripped`, and the diagnosis engine then says "no part to order, flag a pro". That is persona-sensitive, but a reset that changes nothing reads as "ok".
8. Minor: with a rambling caller the agent greeted her as "Harold", the late husband she had just mentioned (`intake_rambling`, one run).
